# Aura Glow

Luxury Candle & Gifting Brand — Website Design & Development Prompt

Design and build a premium, luxurious, globally positioned e-commerce website for a luxury candle and gifting brand.

The brand should communicate:

Luxury

Elegance

Relaxation

Sensory experience

Premium gifting

Sophistication

Exclusivity

Craftsmanship

Modern wealth

Emotional connection

The website should feel like a combination of a high-end fragrance house, luxury fashion brand, boutique hotel, and premium lifestyle brand.

Do NOT make it look like a generic Shopify candle store.

The overall experience should feel expensive, minimal, cinematic, sophisticated, immersive, and memorable.

33. DESIGN PRINCIPLE

The most important requirement:

The website must sell an experience, not just a candle.

Every section should answer one of these questions:

Why should I want this?

Why is this different?

How will this make me feel?

Why is this worth the premium price?

Why would I gift this to someone?

Why should I trust this brand?

The website should create the feeling:

"This isn't just a candle. This is something I want in my home — and something I would be proud to gift."

FINAL EXPERIENCE

The finished website should feel:

Luxury × Sensory × Editorial × Modern × Emotional × Global

It should look like a brand that could eventually sit alongside premium fragrance, fashion, hospitality, and lifestyle brands.

Prioritize brand storytelling, visual hierarchy, premium photography, whitespace, typography, micro-interactions, and conversion.

Do not make the website feel like a template.

Make it feel like a real luxury global brand with a strong visual identity and a premium digital experience.
